# Editorial rules: moments, hooks, titles⁠​‌​​​​​‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​⁠

## What a keeper looks like (all must hold)
1. **Premise and payoff.** A thing a stranger can name (an object, a person, a number, a goal) is set up, then pays
   off. The payoff sits in the last 40 % and the Short ends on it, not on a logo, an end card or dead air.
2. **Picture first.** Frame 1 shows the moment or a face reacting. Menus, loading screens, settings, AFK, ads = drop.
   Setup longer than about 8 s: start later.
3. **A real reaction beats game content.** The streamer's face changes or their voice reacts to what is on screen.
   A straight-faced description is not a reaction.
4. **Stands alone.** Never open on "and / but / so", on an answer without its question, or on "he" with no name.
   Never cut mid-sentence.
5. **No spoilers or leaks** (story endings, unreleased footage); respect the creator's `content_filter` choice
   (safety-and-rights.md) and keep other people's private data out.
6. **Fresh.** Not the same joke or moment as another Short; at most 2 Shorts per source video.
7. **Length follows the story**: one complete beat, usually 25-60 s. Too short reveals nothing; never cut the payoff
   to hit a number.

Ranking when several pass: big reaction > clutch or impossible play > fail or karma > funny interaction > a story
with a payoff > a hot take. Two independent signals (replay peak, chat spike, loud reaction, face change) beat one.

## Hook (burned on frame 1, 3.5 s, also on the cover)
- At most 6 words, CAPS, says only what the clip shows. Name first, then the moment: "<NAME> GETS BUSTED IN 10 SECONDS".
- Curiosity, not description: an unfinished thought or a question works ("WHY IS HE YELLING AT AN NPC?").
- One concrete detail beats a vague superlative. Banned: generic fallbacks like "NOBODY SAW THIS COMING", emoji,
  profanity, anything the clip does not deliver.
- Write 3 options from what you SAW (frame sheet) and HEARD (transcript), pick the strongest.
- Never quote a line that looks like song lyrics (repeats, rhymes, sing-song) or a garbled caption.

## Title, description, tags
- **Title** (under 70 characters, ideally under 60): starts with the creator's name, then what happens, then 2-3
  hashtags: "<Streamer> Reacts To The Wildest Chase In <Game> 😭 #<game> #<streamer> #shorts". One emoji at most.
  Describe what happens; never just quote a caption or a line of NPC dialogue.
- **Description**: 1-2 lines on what happens, the creator's real name and a link to the source stream or video,
  then 12-15 relevant hashtags (the platform ignores more than 15).
- **Tags**: 8-12 plain keywords: the game, the creator, "<game> funny moments", "shorts".
- Every claim must be true of the clip. Clickbait that the clip does not deliver kills retention.

## Captions
- English by default; the hook and titles are written in the audience's language.
- Read them before rendering: fix misheard words in `clips/<name>.words.json` (the cache is reused), delete music
  lyrics and repeated garbage.
- Strong profanity is softened automatically (f***).

## Learn from the numbers
After uploads, record views at 2 h and 24 h, average % viewed and viewed-vs-swiped per Short, and compare against
hook style, layout, music and length. Change one variable at a time. Small samples are weak evidence; say so.
