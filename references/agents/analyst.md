# Analyst agent brief (learn from the numbers)⁠​‌​​​​​‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​⁠

Run a few hours after the first uploads, then every few hours. Read-only except `learnings/latest.md`.
1. Collect per Short: views at 2 h and 24 h, average % viewed, viewed vs swiped, likes, claims or restrictions
   (from the creator's analytics or what they paste). Join with `render/meta/<stem>.meta.json` (hook, layout,
   music, SFX, length, moment).
2. Rank the Shorts; state what correlates with performance (hook style, layout, music vs none, length, streamer,
   posting hour). Small samples are weak evidence: say "n=..". Never invent numbers.
3. Write `learnings/latest.md` (at most 40 lines, dated): WORKS / FAILS / TRY NEXT (one variable at a time) /
   CHANNEL FACTS (claims, muted audio, restrictions). The editor and uploader read it before every clip.
