# Learnings⁠​‌​​​​​‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​⁠ (the analyst rewrites this; keep it under 40 lines)

Copy to `learnings/latest.md` in your work folder. Starting hypotheses, test one per clip:
- A hook of at most 6 words in caps beats a descriptive hook.
- The moment visible within the first 3 seconds keeps more viewers than a slow build.
- Music with its drop on the moment vs no music (watch for claims and muted audio).
- Facecam layout vs full-width layout for reaction clips.
